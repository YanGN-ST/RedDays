Calendar subscriptions for mainland China statutory holidays, Hong Kong public holidays and several calendar systems, updated automatically. The calendars contain red days only — statutory holidays and makeup workdays — without the noise of ordinary weekends.

## Subscribe

In macOS Calendar, choose **File → New Calendar Subscription** (⌥⌘S) and paste a URL. On iPhone or iPad, replace `https://` with `webcal://`, or tap a link on the project page.

| Calendar | Subscription |
|---|---|
| Mainland holidays, detailed | `https://yangn-st.github.io/RedDays/reddays-detailed.ics` |

## Feed variants

| Version | Contents | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Detailed | Every holiday day, plus makeup workdays with 09:00–18:00 times and a 21:00 reminder the night before | Following a holiday day by day and catching makeup days |
| Minimal | Only the first holiday day and makeup workdays, about 13 events a year | Keeping the calendar quiet most of the time |
| Workdays only | Makeup workdays only, with the same times and reminders | People who only need a reminder not to miss a makeup day |

You can subscribe to both the detailed and minimal feeds, keep the minimal one visible most of the year, and switch on the detailed one before a holiday. The workdays-only feed is for people who only want makeup reminders.

## Event format

Titles are short enough to read in month view. They show the holiday, whether the day is a holiday or a makeup workday, and the position within that period:

```text
春节 假 2/9
春节 补 1/2
国庆节 补 2/2
```

Opening an event shows the full context: the holiday range, the makeup workdays, the next holiday and a link to the official notice.

```text
「春节」假期第2天（共9天）
假期：2月15日至2月23日
调休补班：2月14日、2月28日
下一个假期：清明节（4月4日至4月6日）
依据国务院办公厅通知: https://www.gov.cn/...
```

| Calendar | Contents | Source |

| Ethnic festivals | Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Tibetan New Year, Water Splashing Festival, Torch Festival and Yi New Year | Tibetan New Year follows the Tibetan calendar almanac; other dates follow local government notices, with moon-sighting notes where applicable |

## Sources and limits

- Mainland and Hong Kong holidays follow their respective official notices. Lunar dates and solar terms use astronomical calculation.
- Ethnic festival dates come from local sources. Islamic festival dates may differ by a day when official moon-sighting announcements differ from calculation.
- The daily almanac reflects folk traditions and is for reference only.
- Next-year holidays depend on official publication and validation. Calendar refresh intervals are controlled by the subscribing device.

## FAQ

- **Why are there no ordinary weekends?** Weekends that are merely part of a longer break are not statutory holidays, so the official data does not include them. The calendar follows the data.
- **Why is next year empty?** The next year's schedule is published by the State Council, usually between late October and early December. It goes live within about 12 hours of publication after automated validation; a failed check blocks the release and opens an alert issue.
- **Why did my phone not show an update?** iOS refreshes subscriptions on its own schedule, from a few hours to a day. On a Mac, right-click the calendar and choose Refresh. Event UIDs are stable, so removing and re-adding a subscription does not create duplicates.
